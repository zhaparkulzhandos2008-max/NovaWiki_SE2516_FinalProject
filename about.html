06 / OUR STORY

Made for curious minds.

NovaWiki is a student-built educational website that makes the foundations of the web approachable.

OUR PURPOSE

Make the web less intimidating.

We combine simple explanations, visual examples, curated references and small learning milestones. NovaWiki is designed for students and beginners who want a reliable place to start.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Is NovaWiki free?

Yes. Learning materials and demo accounts are free.

Do I need programming experience?

No. The roadmap starts from the fundamentals.

Where is my account data stored?

Only in your current browser using localStorage. No backend or real authentication service is connected.

What if the API is unavailable?

The Live resources page shows a clear error and offers curated reference links.
